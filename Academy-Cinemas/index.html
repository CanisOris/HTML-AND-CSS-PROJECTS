Academy Cinemas

Opening its brass-handled doors in the vibrant autumn of 1924, Academy Cinemas stood as the crown jewel of the city's entertainment district. Originally designed as a lavish vaudeville palace, its iconic Art Deco marquee and velvet-draped walls have witnessed a century of magic. From roaring twenties jazz ensembles and the earliest silver-screen "talkies" to modern independent premieres, this lovingly restored historic landmark continues to preserve the golden age of cinema for generations of dreamers.
