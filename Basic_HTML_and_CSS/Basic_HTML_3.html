Two celestial koi in an ocean of night,
One woven of shadow, the other of light.
They trace out a circle, a perfect embrace,
A whirlpool of spirit suspended in space.

Not water they swim through, but stardust and mass,
Watching the eons of quietness pass.
A single great galaxy, breathing and whole,
Two spinning halves of a singular soul.

They twirl and they tether, the dark and the gleam,
Alive in the current of time’s longest dream.
A devotion that bloomed long before the first spark,
When nothing existed but waiting and dark.

The pearl in the black and the jet in the white,
A balance of gravity, motion, and light.
Older than cosmos, their dance has no end,
Where starlight and shadow eternally blend.
